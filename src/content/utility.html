PayVang can improve discovery and assistance, but it must not independently operate a bill-payment system or collect biller funds outside the authorised framework.

Customer use cases
  • Assisted bill access at a society help desk or through a digital interface.
  • Bill fetch, customer validation, payment status, receipt retrieval and complaint initiation where supported by the authorised partner.
  • Supported categories may include electricity, water, gas, broadband, telecom, DTH, municipal payments, school fees and other categories enabled by the partner and scheme rules.
Permitted operating construct
LayerResponsible partyPayVang boundary
Customer interface Authorised COU directly or through an approved/certified Agent Institution. PayVang interface is used only under a signed and approved partner arrangement.
Bill fetch and validation Bharat Connect/BBPS participant and connected biller. PayVang displays returned information; it does not invent, alter or independently host bill obligations.
Payment and settlement Authorised payment-system participants under scheme rules. No customer or biller funds flow through a PayVang operating account or technology-service-provider account.
Complaint routing COU/BOU/NBBL framework and responsible biller, as applicable. PayVang captures and routes the request, provides status visibility and avoids unsupported resolution promises.

GO-LIVE CONDITION Bill-payment functionality is activated only after partner due diligence, contractual appointment, technical certification where required, approved customer disclosures and end-to-end transaction testing.

Regulatory basis: RBI's 2024 BBPS Directions state that an entity other than a biller operating a bill-payment system outside BBPS requires authorisation, and that funds must not flow through a Technology Service Provider.
