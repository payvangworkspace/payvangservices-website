School fees without aggregation

The school remains the biller and merchant of record; PayVang supplies the workflow, experience and reconciliation layer.

Supported workflows
WorkflowWhat the institution controlsWhat PayVang provides
Fee configuration Fee heads, amounts, due dates, concessions, late-fee rules and student mapping. Configuration interface, import tools, validations and maker-checker workflow.
Parent communication Approved messages, contact policy and escalation route. Invoices, reminders, payment links, receipt notices and help-desk tracking.
Payment acceptance School merchant/biller onboarding and settlement bank account. Technical integration to the school's authorised bank/PA/BOU arrangement.
Reconciliation Books of account, fee ledger and exception approval. Transaction matching, reference mapping, exception queue and downloadable MIS.
Refunds/disputes Refund decision and academic/financial policy. Workflow capture, partner routing, status tracking and parent communication.
Eligible fee and service categories
  • Tuition, admission, examination, transport, hostel, library, laboratory and other institution-approved fee heads.
  • Books, uniforms, ID cards, events, activities or other goods/services only where the responsible seller is clearly disclosed.
  • Recurring and one-time notices with student ID, fee period, due date and institution-generated reference.
Settlement and accounting

The parent pays against a school-issued obligation. The authorised payment partner/acquiring arrangement settles the principal amount directly to the school's designated bank account. PayVang receives its software, implementation or managed-service fee under a separate B2B invoice. PayVang does not net its fee from student funds unless the authorised partner and applicable rules expressly support that construct.

CONTROL OUTCOME The institution retains ownership of the fee receivable, refund decision, student ledger and bank settlement. PayVang retains responsibility for the accuracy, security and availability of its technology and support services.
