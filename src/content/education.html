Overview

Clear billing for every fee head

School is a PayVang merchant line covering school, college and tuition services. We bill the fee heads, issue invoices and receipts, take payment in our name and support the payment journey. Academic records, attendance and classroom delivery stay with the institution.

Parent support

What is included

Fee and service categories we bill

  • Tuition, admission, examination and term fees billed by PayVang.
  • Transport, hostel, library, laboratory and activity heads where offered.
  • Books, uniforms, ID cards and event packages where PayVang is the disclosed seller.
  • Recurring and one-time notices with student ID, fee period and due date.
  • Parent invoices, reminders, payment links, receipts and help-desk tracking.
  • Refund and dispute workflow against the invoice, subject to academic policy.
How a school payment works

From fee schedule to receipt

  1. The institution shares fee heads, amounts, due dates and student mapping.
  2. PayVang issues the parent invoice in its own name for the service sold.
  3. The parent pays PayVang through the authorised acquiring arrangement.
  4. PayVang issues the receipt and updates the fee status shared with the institution.
  5. Exceptions, concessions and refunds follow academic policy and our merchant terms.

Control outcome

The parent pays PayVang as merchant for the school service purchased, and settlement follows PayVang's authorised acquiring arrangement. PayVang is responsible for price, delivery, support and refund of the school services it sells, while academic records remain with the institution.
