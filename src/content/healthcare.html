Healthcare bills

Not an independent PayVang payment system

PayVang is not a Payment Aggregator, Payment Gateway or independent bill-payment system. It does not operate a healthcare collections platform or pool patient funds for onward settlement to hospitals or clinics.

Where a medical or related bill category is enabled, access is only through an authorised Bharat Connect/BBPS participant or approved agent-institution model, under the same essential-services construct used for electricity, gas, water, telecom, DTH, broadband and municipal payments.

GO-LIVE CONDITION Bill-payment functionality is activated only after partner due diligence, contractual appointment, technical certification where required, approved customer disclosures and end-to-end transaction testing.
