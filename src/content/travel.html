Overview

The booking desk is ours, not a middleman's

Travel is a PayVang merchant line covering leisure and business journeys. The invoice and receipt show PayVang as the merchant, so fare rules, changes and refunds are handled by us. Where a carrier, hotel or operator fulfils the flight or the stay, PayVang remains the contracting merchant for the package we sold.

Flights

Domestic and international bookings with itinerary, e-ticket reference and a PayVang tax invoice.

Hotels

Stay bookings with a PayVang confirmation showing dates, occupancy and the cancellation window.

Holiday packages

Travel, stay and selected ground services combined into one itinerary and one receipt.

Ground transport

Transfers, rail or coach where offered, with pickup or boarding details confirmed by PayVang.

How a booking works

From quote to return

  1. The traveller chooses flight, hotel, package or transport from PayVang.
  2. PayVang shares a booking summary with fare, taxes, cancellation rules and total.
  3. Payment is taken through PayVang's authorised acquiring arrangement.
  4. PayVang issues the confirmation, tickets or vouchers for the product sold.
  5. Amendments and refunds follow the disclosed fare rules and our merchant terms.

Merchant position

PayVang is responsible for the travel product it sells: price, confirmation, support and refund under the published fare and package rules. Operating carriers and hotels fulfil the journey; the customer's contract for the PayVang-sold booking is with PayVang.
