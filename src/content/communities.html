The PayVang Society Service Desk

A digitally managed B2B2C service layer for residents, facility teams and community administrators.

Operating proposition

PayVang contracts with a society, RWA or facility manager to run a branded digital service desk. Residents discover services, raise requests, receive quotations, book time slots, track fulfilment and obtain support from one interface. The contract defines whether PayVang is the principal service provider or only the technology/managed-service provider.

Service clusterIllustrative scopeControl point
Home care Plumbing, electrical, deep cleaning, pest control, appliance care and minor repairs. PayVang accepts payment only when it is contractually responsible as principal merchant.
Community operations Visitor/parking workflows, amenity booking, notices, polls, help-desk tickets and move coordination. Service subscription is invoiced to the society or facility manager.
Facility programmes AMC scheduling, fire/lift service coordination, waste and housekeeping oversight, preventive-maintenance calendars. Approved subcontractors work under defined SLA and escalation terms.
Society dues Maintenance invoices, reminders, receipts, arrears reports and finance-team reconciliation. Society remains merchant/biller and receives direct settlement.
Resident journey
  1. Resident selects the society and service, or opens a society-issued invoice.
  2. The interface identifies the responsible merchant/biller before payment.
  3. The authorised payment partner presents available payment methods and authenticates the transaction.
  4. The service order or institution invoice updates with the partner reference number.
  5. PayVang tracks fulfilment, communications, service evidence and closure; financial reconciliation follows the configured direct-settlement model.

VALUE TO THE SOCIETY Fewer manual follow-ups, a single resident support trail, service-level visibility, cleaner reconciliation and better accountability without the society surrendering control of its receivables.
