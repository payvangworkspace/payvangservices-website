Overview

A named merchant for your workforce package

Manpower is a PayVang merchant line. We sell the staffing package, invoice in our name, deploy the agreed roles and stay accountable for the contracted service, attendance reporting and support. The client's contract for the PayVang-sold manpower is with PayVang, not with an unnamed labour desk.

Temporary and contract

Office support and back-office roles on shift plans, with attendance reports and cycle invoicing.

Facility and housekeeping

Housekeeping, cleaning and security-support crews with a site roster and completion notes.

Retail and event crews

Floor staff and event teams for defined deployment windows, with an on-site supervisor contact.

Project and skilled roles

Skilled and semi-skilled roles engaged against a scope of work with milestone invoicing.

What is included

How an engagement works

From requirement to invoice

  1. The client shares role, count, shift, location and duration with PayVang.
  2. PayVang issues a commercial proposal and invoice in its own name.
  3. Payment is taken as agreed: advance, cycle or milestone.
  4. PayVang deploys the contracted manpower and shares attendance reports.
  5. Replacements, extensions and refunds follow the disclosed service terms.

Merchant position

PayVang is responsible for price, deployment, support and refund of manpower services it sells. Payment rails are supplied by authorised partners; customer funds for these sales settle to PayVang as merchant.
