Visitor and parking workflows

Community operations under the PayVang Society Service Desk

Visitor and parking workflows sit inside high-rise community operations. The service subscription is invoiced to the society or facility manager. PayVang does not collect parking fees from residents on behalf of unrelated operators under a generic PayVang merchant ID.

Control point
  • Visitor/parking workflows, amenity booking, notices, polls, help-desk tickets and move coordination.
  • Service subscription is invoiced to the society or facility manager.
  • Where a parking or amenity charge is an institution-owned receivable, the society remains merchant/biller and receives direct settlement.

VALUE TO THE SOCIETY Fewer manual follow-ups, a single resident support trail, service-level visibility, cleaner reconciliation and better accountability without the society surrendering control of its receivables.
