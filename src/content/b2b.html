B2B Service Commerce & Direct-Settlement Enablement

One operating layer for high-rise communities, education institutions and everyday service payments.

COMMUNITIES | EDUCATION | ESSENTIAL SERVICES

BUSINESS POSITION PayVang is presented as a B2B merchant and service-technology company - not as a Payment Aggregator, Payment Gateway, BBPOU or independent bill-payment system. Regulated payment acceptance and bill-payment rails are supplied by appropriately authorised partners; PayVang does not pool, hold or settle third-party customer funds.

01 | Corporate Snapshot

A merchant-led service business

PayVang helps institutions digitise service delivery, billing, customer communication and reconciliation without positioning itself as a payment intermediary. The operating model separates the commercial service layer from the regulated movement of money.

The problem we solve
  • Fragmented service vendors, manual help desks and disconnected billing create poor customer experience and weak accountability.
  • Societies and schools often reconcile receipts across multiple channels with limited transaction-level visibility.
  • Users want one interface, but the underlying institution must remain clearly identified as the merchant or biller where it owns the receivable.
  • Banks and payment partners need clean role boundaries, traceable transactions, transparent customer communication and controlled integrations.

PAYVANG'S ANSWER A single service catalogue, invoice and reminder engine, assisted support desk, payment-partner integration, fulfilment controls, reconciliation dashboards and auditable customer-service workflows.

Service design principles
  • One clearly named merchant or biller on every invoice, payment page, receipt and support journey.
  • No vendor marketplace or sub-merchant onboarding under a generic PayVang merchant ID.

  • No regulatory, network or certification claim unless supported by a current, verifiable approval or certificate.

03 | High-rise Communities

The PayVang Society Service Desk

A digitally managed B2B2C service layer for residents, facility teams and community administrators.

Operating proposition

PayVang contracts with a society, RWA or facility manager to run a branded digital service desk. Residents discover services, raise requests, receive quotations, book time slots, track fulfilment and obtain support from one interface. The contract defines whether PayVang is the principal service provider or only the technology/managed-service provider.

Service clusterIllustrative scopeControl point
Home care Plumbing, electrical, deep cleaning, pest control, appliance care and minor repairs. PayVang accepts payment only when it is contractually responsible as principal merchant.
Community operations Visitor/parking workflows, amenity booking, notices, polls, help-desk tickets and move coordination. Service subscription is invoiced to the society or facility manager.
Facility programmes AMC scheduling, fire/lift service coordination, waste and housekeeping oversight, preventive-maintenance calendars. Approved subcontractors work under defined SLA and escalation terms.
Society dues Maintenance invoices, reminders, receipts, arrears reports and finance-team reconciliation. Society remains merchant/biller and receives direct settlement.
Resident journey
  1. Resident selects the society and service, or opens a society-issued invoice.
  2. The interface identifies the responsible merchant/biller before payment.
  3. The authorised payment partner presents available payment methods and authenticates the transaction.
  4. The service order or institution invoice updates with the partner reference number.
  5. PayVang tracks fulfilment, communications, service evidence and closure; financial reconciliation follows the configured direct-settlement model.

VALUE TO THE SOCIETY Fewer manual follow-ups, a single resident support trail, service-level visibility, cleaner reconciliation and better accountability without the society surrendering control of its receivables.

School fees without aggregation

The school remains the biller and merchant of record; PayVang supplies the workflow, experience and reconciliation layer.

Eligible fee and service categories
  • Tuition, admission, examination, transport, hostel, library, laboratory and other institution-approved fee heads.
  • Books, uniforms, ID cards, events, activities or other goods/services only where the responsible seller is clearly disclosed.
  • Recurring and one-time notices with student ID, fee period, due date and institution-generated reference.
Settlement and accounting

The parent pays against a school-issued obligation. The authorised payment partner/acquiring arrangement settles the principal amount directly to the school's designated bank account. PayVang receives its software, implementation or managed-service fee under a separate B2B invoice. PayVang does not net its fee from student funds unless the authorised partner and applicable rules expressly support that construct.

CONTROL OUTCOME The institution retains ownership of the fee receivable, refund decision, student ledger and bank settlement. PayVang retains responsibility for the accuracy, security and availability of its technology and support services.

05 | Essential Services

Bill payment access through authorised rails

PayVang can improve discovery and assistance, but it must not independently operate a bill-payment system or collect biller funds outside the authorised framework.

Customer use cases
  • Assisted bill access at a society help desk or through a digital interface.
  • Bill fetch, customer validation, payment status, receipt retrieval and complaint initiation where supported by the authorised partner.
  • Supported categories may include electricity, water, gas, broadband, telecom, DTH, municipal payments, school fees and other categories enabled by the partner and scheme rules.
Permitted operating construct
LayerResponsible partyPayVang boundary
Customer interface Authorised COU directly or through an approved/certified Agent Institution. PayVang interface is used only under a signed and approved partner arrangement.
Bill fetch and validation Bharat Connect/BBPS participant and connected biller. PayVang displays returned information; it does not invent, alter or independently host bill obligations.
Payment and settlement Authorised payment-system participants under scheme rules. No customer or biller funds flow through a PayVang operating account or technology-service-provider account.

Regulatory basis: RBI's 2024 BBPS Directions state that an entity other than a biller operating a bill-payment system outside BBPS requires authorisation, and that funds must not flow through a Technology Service Provider.

06 | Transaction Architecture

Three clean commercial flows

The model is selected before onboarding and remains visible throughout invoicing, payment, settlement, reconciliation and customer support.

Flow A

PayVang as principal merchant

Customer -> authorised acquirer/payment partner -> PayVang merchant settlement account -> PayVang fulfils or controls the contracted service

  • Use only for goods/services sold by PayVang in its own name and under its own customer terms.
  • PayVang controls price, invoice, fulfilment, support, cancellation and refund responsibility.
Flow B

Institution as merchant/biller

Resident/parent -> authorised partner -> society/school bank account -> PayVang receives separate B2B service fee

  • Use for society maintenance, school fees and institution-owned receivables.
  • PayVang does not receive, pool, split or onward-settle the institution's principal funds.
Flow C

Bill payment through Bharat Connect/BBPS

Customer -> authorised COU/Agent Institution interface -> BBPS/NBBL rails -> connected biller

  • Use only after PayVang is accepted in the appropriate partner role and the channel is certified/approved as required.
  • The BBPS reference and partner-led dispute framework remain authoritative.
Mandatory transaction data
Before paymentAfter paymentFor reconciliation

Partner transaction reference; status; date/time; payer reference; receipt source; support route. Invoice ID; institution/service ID; settlement reference; exception code; refund/dispute status; audit timestamps.

07 | Commercial Model

How PayVang earns revenue

Revenue is linked to contracted services and technology - not to unauthorised possession or onward settlement of customer funds.

Revenue streamBasisTypical payer
Implementation fee Configuration, data mapping, integration, training and go-live support. Society, school, enterprise customer or contracted partner.
Platform subscription Monthly/annual SaaS access by site, institution, module or user tier. Institution or enterprise customer.
Managed-service fee Help desk, billing operations, communication, reconciliation support and service-level management. Institution, facility manager or enterprise customer.
Principal-service revenue Price of a PayVang-owned service package, including disclosed taxes and any lawful charges. Resident, parent, institution or enterprise buyer.
Fulfilment margin Margin retained when PayVang is the principal merchant and subcontractors fulfil under PayVang's responsibility. Embedded in the PayVang service price.
Partner service fee Technology, lead-management or support fee under a written B2B agreement, where permitted. Authorised partner or institution; never disguised as held customer funds.
Explicitly excluded revenue constructs
  • Interest or float earned on customer, school, society or biller funds.
  • Settlement spread created by retaining funds before remitting them to a third party.
  • Onboarding unrelated sub-merchants to accept payments under PayVang's merchant credentials.
  • Undisclosed convenience, handling or service charges.
  • Revenue dependent on describing PayVang as an authorised payment-system operator when it is not one.

COMMERCIAL GOVERNANCE Every proposal identifies the service owner, merchant/biller of record, settlement beneficiary, invoice issuer, fee payer, tax treatment, refund owner and complaint owner before the first live transaction.

10 | Partnership Model

Roles, responsibilities and onboarding

Clear accountability enables faster bank/partner review and safer scale across multiple institutions.

ActivityPayVangInstitution / billerAuthorised partner
Commercial service Defines and fulfils PayVang-owned services; supports institution workflows. Owns institution services, fees and underlying receivables. Not applicable unless separately contracted.
Merchant/biller onboarding Provides its own KYC and required technical information. Provides entity, bank and fee/biller data for direct onboarding. Performs regulated merchant/biller due diligence and approval.

Institution onboarding sequence
  1. Confirm legal entity, use case, merchant/biller of record and exact fund flow.
  2. Complete KYC, bank-account validation, beneficial-owner checks and service-category review through the authorised partner where applicable.
  3. Execute commercial, data-processing, security, SLA, refund and complaint-responsibility agreements.
  4. Configure catalogue/fees, users, branding, settlement beneficiary and integration credentials in test mode.
  5. Perform positive, failed, reversed, refund and reconciliation test cases; approve disclosures and support scripts.
  6. Go live with monitoring, daily exception review and scheduled access/security/commercial reviews.
