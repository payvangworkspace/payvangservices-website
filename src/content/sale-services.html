Overview

A single named seller for every order

Sale Services is PayVang's retail and commercial line. We set the price, take the order, deliver the product or package, and handle support and refunds under our own terms. PayVang does not onboard other sellers under a generic merchant ID, so if a product is listed, PayVang is the seller.

Retail goods

Catalogue and SKU-based goods billed and shipped by PayVang, with a tax invoice and delivery confirmation for every order.

Service packages

Maintenance, installation, warranty add-ons and on-site visits sold as PayVang products with a defined scope and completion note.

Subscriptions

Recurring plans with a PayVang invoice each billing cycle, renewal notices and a clear cancellation path.

How a sale works

From order to closure

  1. The customer selects a catalogue item, package or subscription from PayVang.
  2. PayVang issues an invoice in its own name with amount, taxes and delivery terms.
  3. Payment is taken through PayVang's authorised acquiring arrangement.
  4. PayVang fulfils the order or schedules the service and shares status updates.
  5. Support, returns and refunds are raised against the PayVang invoice.

PayVang is responsible for price, delivery, support and refund of Sale Services it sells. Payment rails are supplied by authorised partners; customer funds for these sales settle to PayVang as merchant.
