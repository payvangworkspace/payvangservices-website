Grievance Redressal

Objectives:
The objective of the policy is to ensure that:

  1. All customers are treated fairly at all times.
  2. All complaints, critical requests and issues raised by customers are dealt with courtesy and resolved on time.
  3. Customers are made completely aware of their rights so that they can opt for alternative remedies if they are not fully satisfied with our response or resolution to their complaint.
Redressal mechanism for grievances

Level 1

Online queries via email – info@payvang.com

First response time – 2 working days

Resolution Time
  • PayVang’s internal resolution time – 4 working days
  • Resolution time does not include time taken by the customer to provide required information/documentation.
  • If any case/transaction is linked to other bank then the TAT will be dependent on the respective banks /network providers/regulator. Resolution time will include this TAT along with PayVang’s internal resolution time.
  • If in any case additional time is needed, PayVang will inform the customer the reasons for the delay and provide expected timelines for resolution of the issue.
