How it works

The same clear process for every service

Whatever you buy from us, you always know who the seller is, what the price covers and who to contact afterwards.

  1. Choose the service you need from the PayVang catalogue.
  2. Receive a PayVang invoice showing price, taxes and terms.
  3. Pay securely through our authorised payment partner.
  4. We deliver the service and share confirmation or status.
  5. Support, changes and refunds are raised against your invoice.
Named merchant PayVang is shown as the seller on every invoice and receipt.
Secure payments Payment rails supplied by appropriately authorised partners.
Clear terms Price, taxes, delivery and refund terms disclosed before you pay.
One support desk A single team owns your order from purchase through to closure.
