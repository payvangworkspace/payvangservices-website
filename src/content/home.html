Transaction architecture

Three clean commercial flows

The model is selected before onboarding and stays visible through invoicing, payment, settlement, reconciliation and support.

Flow A

PayVang as principal merchant

Customer → authorised partner → PayVang settlement account. Used only for goods and services sold by PayVang in its own name.

Flow B

Institution as merchant/biller

Resident or parent → authorised partner → society or school bank account. PayVang receives a separate B2B service fee.

Flow C

Bharat Connect / BBPS

Customer → authorised COU or Agent Institution → BBPS rails → connected biller. No bill-payment funds pass through PayVang.

How a journey works

The same control gates every time

The interface identifies the responsible merchant or biller before payment. Final status comes from the authorised partner, not from an unverified success screen.

  1. The resident or parent selects a service, or opens a society- or school-issued invoice.
  2. The interface names the responsible merchant or biller before payment continues.
  3. The authorised payment partner presents methods and authenticates the transaction.
  4. The order or invoice updates with the partner reference number.
  5. PayVang tracks fulfilment, support and reconciliation under the configured settlement model.
